# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to OctoAcme’s centralized project management knowledge hub. This documentation standardizes how the team defines, plans, delivers, and improves work across initiatives. The goal is to make project management practices visible, repeatable, and easy to navigate so that teams can move from idea to delivery with fewer surprises and less dependence on individual tribal knowledge.

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle that begins with initiation, moves into planning and execution, and closes with release and continuous improvement. New work is validated through a clear business case and stakeholder alignment before a lightweight project one-pager is created. Once approved, the team turns ideas into a backlog with acceptance criteria, milestones, dependencies, and a shared understanding of the work to be delivered. This approach keeps planning concrete while preserving flexibility to adapt as new information emerges.

The operating model is built around clear ownership and communication. Product managers define the problem, success metrics, and priorities; project managers coordinate schedules, risks, and stakeholder communication; developers build and validate solutions; QA ensures quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide alignment and approvals. Regular standups, weekly syncs, milestone reviews, and structured escalation paths keep work visible and help teams respond quickly when risks or blockers appear. OctoAcme also emphasizes quality assurance through CI checks, testing requirements, security scans, review gates, and smoke tests before release.

The result is a consistent, cross-functional process that supports predictable execution without sacrificing agility. By pairing role clarity with defined workflows, measurable outcomes, and strong communication practices, OctoAcme helps teams deliver value incrementally while continuously learning from each sprint, release, and retrospective.
